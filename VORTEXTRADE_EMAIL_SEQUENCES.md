## Tips for Email Success

1. **Personalization** - Use the user's first name and reference their activity
2. **Timing** - Send emails at optimal times (Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM)
3. **Clear CTA** - Every email should have one clear call-to-action
4. **Mobile-friendly** - Ensure emails look good on phones
5. **Value first** - Provide value before asking for anything
6. **Test subject lines** - A/B test to find what works best
7. **Segment users** - Send different emails based on user behavior
8. **Track metrics** - Monitor open rates, click rates, conversions
