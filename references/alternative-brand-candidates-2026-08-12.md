# Alternative Brand Candidates — Initial Screen

## Naming criteria

Candidates aim to avoid the crowded **Vortex / Trade / AI** construction; sound credible for an evidence-led signal and paper-trading workspace; avoid return or execution promises; and support a descriptive qualifier such as **“Signals for Trading 212”** without becoming generic.

## Candidates selected for the initial public-web screen

| Candidate | Suggested public descriptor | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Luminara Signals** | Evidence-led signals for Trading 212 | Bright, analytical association without promising outcomes. |
| **Northstar Ledger** | Signals and paper-trading workspace | Navigation and record-keeping associations; differs from automated-execution language. |
| **Signalloom** | A structured market-signal workspace | Evokes bringing separate indicators together. |
| **Axiomarc** | Rules-led market analysis | Suggests transparent reasoning and an analytical process. |
| **Tensile Market** | Market signals and simulator | Suggests disciplined resilience rather than return claims. |
| **Kestrel Signal** | Investor signal workspace | A memorable natural-world name plus a restrained product descriptor. |
| **Mosaic Ledger** | AI-supported market research | Highlights combining inputs and recording decisions. |
| **Clearpath Signals** | Paper-trading and signal analysis | Emphasises clarity, although it may be more descriptive than distinctive. |
| **Halcyon Market** | Evidence-led market intelligence | Calm, premium tone; requires strong product descriptor. |
| **StrataSignal** | Technical signals and paper trading | Directly expresses multi-layer analysis while remaining more distinctive than Vortextrade. |

## Screening limit

This is a creative shortlist only. A public-web search can identify obvious conflicts, but it cannot establish domain availability, trademark availability, company-name availability, or legal clearance.
