# Premium Coined One-Word Name Batch — Draft 1

> **Purpose:** Develop a more ownable parent brand that communicates performance through its tone, while a descriptive tagline explains the product. No candidate should be treated as cleared until trademark, domain, company-name, and social-handle checks are complete.

## Screening Method

Each candidate will receive an initial public-web screen for existing exact or near-exact uses, with special attention to financial services, investment products, trading platforms, market-data tools, financial software, and adjacent regulated categories. The screen will identify obvious category conflict only; it does not constitute legal clearance.
