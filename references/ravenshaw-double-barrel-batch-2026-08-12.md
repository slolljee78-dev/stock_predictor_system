# Ravenshaw Double-Barrel Brand Batch — Draft 1

> **Naming approach:** Use `Ravenshaw` as the distinctive anchor, paired with a concise, familiar British surname or landscape word. The second word should add rhythm and memorability, not repeat financial language. The public-facing brand can use an ampersand; any future legal entity name should be confirmed separately after formal clearance.

## Design Rules

The construction should feel British, premium, composed, and consumer-facing rather than like a traditional wealth-management partnership. It must avoid `Trade`, `Trading`, `Finance`, performance claims, and numbers. The product purpose will be carried by a factual descriptor: **“AI stock signals and paper-trading tools.”**

| Candidate | Intended tone |

| Ravenshaw & Reed | Precise, composed, and analytical. |
| Ravenshaw & Finch | Alert, compact, and memorable. |
| Ravenshaw & Holt | Natural strength and an English estate-like tone. |
| Ravenshaw & March | Purposeful, forward-looking movement. |

| Ravenshaw & Birch | Grounded, premium, and recognisably British. |
| Ravenshaw & Beck | Short, clean, and landscape-rooted. |
| Ravenshaw & Sloane | Refined and metropolitan. |

| Ravenshaw & Vale | Calm, premium, and confident. |

## Screening Scope

The initial public-web screen will examine exact and close uses in UK finance, investment, trading, fintech, market data, technology, and related regulated categories. It does not constitute trademark, company-name, domain, or legal clearance.

## Initial Public-Web Screen

| Candidate | Relevant finding | Initial assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Ravenshaw & Reed | No direct full-name conflict surfaced in the initial finance and technology screen. | Strong initial candidate. |
| Ravenshaw & Finch | Finch Capital is an active European business- and financial-technology investor. | Avoid. |
| Ravenshaw & Holt | Holt is used in company valuation and fintech-investment contexts. | Avoid. |
| Ravenshaw & March | No direct full-name conflict surfaced; the construction remains clear and energetic. | Strong initial candidate. |
| Ravenshaw & Rowe | `Rowe` is closely associated with major investment manager T. Rowe Price. | Medium risk; not preferred. |
| Ravenshaw & Birch | Birch has personal-finance and trade-finance associations. | Medium risk; not preferred. |
| Ravenshaw & Beck | No direct full-name finance or technology conflict surfaced in the initial screen. | Strong initial candidate. |
| Ravenshaw & Sloane | No direct full-name conflict surfaced; it is polished but risks feeling more like a wealth-management partnership. | Medium risk; viable subject to preference. |
| Ravenshaw & Mercer | Mercer is a major financial-planning and wealth-management brand. | Exclude. |
| Ravenshaw & Vale | Vale has multiple existing UK financial-services uses. | Avoid. |

## Preliminary Shortlist

The leading alternatives are **Ravenshaw & Reed**, **Ravenshaw & Beck**, and **Ravenshaw & March**. The first is the most composed and premium; the second is the cleanest and most modern; the third carries the clearest sense of momentum but is slightly less surname-like.

## Product Descriptor Application

The recommended lock-up avoids loading regulatory meaning into the name:

| Brand lock-up | Use |
|---|---|
| **Ravenshaw & Reed** | Parent brand in navigation, app, and marketing. |
| **AI stock signals and paper-trading tools** | Plain-language descriptor beneath the brand and in metadata. |
| **Signal Engine** | Core product name for the existing paper-trading workspace. |
