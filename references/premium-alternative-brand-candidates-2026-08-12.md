# Premium Alternative Brand Direction — Initial Candidates

## Brand character

The new direction should feel closer to a premium investment-research or private-client intelligence platform than to a retail trading bot. Names should therefore be short, abstract or lightly Latinate, calm, precise, and credible; they should not include **trade**, **signal**, **AI**, **quant**, **vortex**, **auto**, or explicit performance language.

The public product descriptor can carry clarity, for example **“Evidence-led signals for Trading 212”**, while the master brand remains distinctive and ownable.
