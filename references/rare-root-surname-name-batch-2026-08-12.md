# Rare-Root British Surname Name Batch — Draft 1

> **Aim:** Preserve the classic British authority, strong consonants, and visual silhouette that worked in Thornwick and Greymark, while using less-public roots that are less likely to overlap with finance, trading, fintech, and AI businesses.

## Naming Rules

Each candidate has two or three syllables, a clear English-sounding structure, and a root that can plausibly read as a surname or place without borrowing ordinary investment vocabulary. The batch excludes familiar finance words, generic technology words, direct animal names, and numbers. The platform remains clearly described as **“AI stock signals and paper-trading tools.”**

| Candidate | Intended character |
|---|---|
| Tarnwick | Direct, sharp, and performance-minded. |
| Marlwick | Darker, more premium, and estate-like. |
| Harrowell | Composed, classic, and distinctly British. |
| Dunmere | Quietly confident with a strong landscape feel. |
| Alderwick | Crafted, established, and refined. |
| Brackenwell | Richer, more traditional, and memorable. |
| Wethercroft | Heritage-led, dependable, and distinctive. |
| Rookridge | Dramatic and more assertive without using a simple animal name. |
| Barrowmere | Grounded, strong, and long-term in tone. |
| Ashenwick | Dark, polished, and close in character to Ravenshaw. |

## Screening Scope

The early screen will look for exact and close use across UK and international finance, investment, trading, fintech, market data, artificial intelligence, technology, and regulated services. Candidates with direct same- or adjacent-category uses will be excluded immediately. This is not legal, trademark, domain, or company-name clearance.

## Provisional Recommendation

**Ashenwick** is the lead candidate. It has the desired British surname silhouette, darker premium character, and no direct category conflict found in the early screen. **Dunmere** is the best alternative if the brand should feel a little more refined and lifestyle-led.

## Method Note

The screen used exact and close name searches against finance, investment, trading, fintech, AI, and technology contexts, plus UK company-record signals. An absence of an early public-web result is not proof of availability. Formal trademark, company-name, domain, and legal clearance remain required before adoption.
