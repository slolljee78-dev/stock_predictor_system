## Provisional Outcome

The **surname/place route is stronger** than heritage/architecture. It gives the platform a more natural British premium tone without relying on generic finance vocabulary. **Ravenshaw Seven** is the cleanest initial screen result; **Thornley Nine** is the strongest in natural British character, but its overlap with an established premium automotive brand makes it less clean.

Neither candidate should be adopted yet. The right next step is a small, more focused batch built around the qualities of **Ravenshaw Seven**—distinctive English-sounding surname, strong consonants, and a single digit—rather than another broad exploration.
