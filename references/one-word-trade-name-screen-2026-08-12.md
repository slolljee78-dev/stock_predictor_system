# One-Word + Trade/Trading Candidate Screen — Initial Results

> **Legal note:** This is an initial public-web screen only. It is not trademark, company-name, domain, social-handle, or legal clearance.

## Result

All twelve shortlisted common English one-word candidates have **high initial confusion risk** in the Vortextrade category once finance and Trade/Trading extensions are considered.

| Candidate | Key overlapping use | Why it is unsuitable |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | PACE Trader / Pace Stock Broking | Direct trading and finance use. |
| Stride | Stride Trade | Active behavioural trading terminal. |
| Summit | Summit Financial / Summit Trading | Wealth-management and broker-dealer use. |
| Forge | Forge Global | Active private-share marketplace and financial-data platform. |
| Apex | Apex Fintech / Apex Trader Funding | Direct fintech and trading-platform uses. |
| Metric | MetricTrade | Direct trading-journal and trading-tool use. |
| Proof | Proof Trading | Institutional equities execution platform. |
| Fathom | Fathom HQ / Fathom Trade | Finance software and investment-firm use. |
| Crest | CREST settlement system | Major UK securities-settlement association. |
| Vector | Vector Trading / Vector Trade Capital | Direct trading and finance uses. |
| Atlas | Atlas Financial / Atlas Trade AI | Direct wealth-management and algorithmic-trading uses. |
| Keystone | KeyStone Financial / Keystone Trading | Investment-research and trading-group uses. |

## Implication

Adding **Trade** or **Trading** makes an already common word *less* viable: it leads searchers straight into pre-existing financial and trading brands. A viable future name will likely need a distinctive coined word or a more unusual real-word construction, with a descriptive tagline—not `Trade`—carrying the product explanation.
