# Ashenwick vs Dunmere — Focused Head-to-Head Screen

## Focused Findings

| Candidate | Creative fit | Focused search result | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ashenwick** | Dark, classic, dramatic, and closest to the character that made Ravenshaw appealing. | Searches surfaced a photography/videography creator, fictional/location uses, and a small collectible-artefact social account, but no direct finance, investing, trading, fintech, market-data, AI, or UK-company conflict. | The only viable finalist in this comparison, subject to formal clearance. |
| **Dunmere** | Refined, premium, and more lifestyle-led. | Dunmere Capital is an active private investment firm; Dunmere LLC operates an ERP platform from London; a historical UK Dunmere Investments entity also exists. | Do not adopt. The investment and technology conflicts are too close. |

## Recommended Brand Application

| Element | Recommended application |
|---|---|
| Parent brand | **Ashenwick** |
| Public descriptor | **AI stock signals and paper-trading tools** |
| Core product | **Ashenwick Signal Engine** |
| Short product descriptor | **Signals, paper trades, and disciplined market research.** |

The parent name remains separate from regulated terminology. The descriptor makes the product understandable without calling it `Trade`, `Trading`, `Finance`, or making performance claims.

## Recommendation

**Ashenwick is the recommendation from this head-to-head.** It has the stronger creative fit and did not show a direct category conflict in the focused screen. It must still be checked for trademark classes, Companies House naming, domains, social handles, and qualified legal clearance before any public rename.
