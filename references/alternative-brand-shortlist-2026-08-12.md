# Alternative Brand Shortlist — Initial Public-Web Screen

**Prepared:** 12 August 2026

> **Legal note:** This is an initial public-web screen, not trademark, company-name, or domain-availability clearance. A qualified intellectual-property lawyer should clear any preferred candidate before a launch decision.

## Recommended shortlist

| Rank | Candidate | Initial public-web risk | Why it is a stronger fit |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mosaic Ledger** | Low | The phrase surfaced primarily in non-finance sectors. It supports the product’s evidence and record-keeping proposition without promising returns or automated execution. |
| 2 | **Kestrel Signal** | Low | Existing uses found were in RF and wireless analytics rather than financial services. It is memorable and can be paired with a clear product descriptor. |
| 3 | **Halcyon Market** | Medium | Existing uses are mainly hospitality/retail rather than finance. The premium tone is strong, but a formal clearance is essential before selection. |
| 4 | **Axiomarc** | Medium | It has no major finance overlap in the initial screen, but it is close to the established gaming term “Axiom Arc.” |
| 5 | **Signalloom** | Medium | It has no trading overlap in the screen, but an existing AI governance product uses the close name Signal Loom. |

## Candidates removed from consideration

| Candidate | Reason not to progress |
|---|---|
| Luminara Signals | Direct active trading-signal use. |
| Clearpath Signals | Direct forex-signal use. |
| StrataSignal | Existing finance/analytics product use. |
| Northstar Ledger | Existing bookkeeping and advisory overlap. |
| Tensile Market | Existing trading-education and industrial-market associations. |

## Suggested public descriptors

Use a consistent descriptor only after clearance, for example:

- **Mosaic Ledger — Evidence-led signals for Trading 212**
- **Kestrel Signal — Market signals and paper-trading workspace**
- **Halcyon Market — Evidence-led market intelligence**

## Required decision sequence

1. Choose two preferred candidates from the shortlist.
2. Check exact domains and social handles.
3. Obtain formal trademark and company-name clearance in the intended launch markets.
4. Only then decide whether a controlled rename is warranted before paid launch.
