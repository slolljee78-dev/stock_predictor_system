## Interpretation

Neither direction currently produces a safe initial shortlist. The candidates resonate more naturally with a British audience than the earlier coined-word batch, but they are already occupied in the very category that Vortextrade serves. Adding `Trade`, `Trading`, or `Finance` would increase—not solve—the confusion.

The workable next direction is a **distinctive but familiar British construction**: either a less-common English or regional word, or a compact two-word structure, paired with a factual descriptor such as **“AI stock signals and paper-trading tools.”**
