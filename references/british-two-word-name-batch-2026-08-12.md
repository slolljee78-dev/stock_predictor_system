# British Two-Word Brand Batch — Draft 1

> **Naming approach:** Pair a natural, recognisably British-rooted word with a simple single digit. This preserves the plainness and memorability of a name such as Trading 212 without echoing its format, while creating more distinct search territory than a common standalone word.

## Design Rules

The candidates must be easy to pronounce, read naturally in British English, avoid financial jargon, avoid `Trade` and `Trading`, and feel more like a premium consumer brand than a fintech acronym. The digit should function as a signature, not a promise, rating, or performance claim.

## Screening Scope

The public-web screen will look for exact or near-exact uses of the full two-word construction, with emphasis on UK financial services, investing, trading, fintech, market data, technology, and regulated categories. It does not constitute trademark, company-name, domain, or legal clearance.

## Provisional Outcome

The full two-word construction improves distinctiveness, but this batch does not yet yield a strong, clear winner. **Moor Eight** has the cleanest initial screen but may not carry enough premium performance character. **Cairn Seven**, **Cedar Eight**, and **Grove Nine** are the closest tonal fits, but all require stronger formal clearance because their leading word already appears in financial contexts.
