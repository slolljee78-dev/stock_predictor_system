# Coined Root + Number Candidates — Initial Screen

## Criteria

The candidates use two or three syllables, avoid obvious financial terminology, and pair a coined root with a restrained number to preserve the concise confidence of the preferred direction. They are intended to feel more premium and ownable than literal directional phrases.

| Candidate | Character |

## Limitation

All names are creative candidates only. They require public-web, domain, company-name, social-handle, trademark, and legal review before any brand decision.
