# Thornwick vs Greymark — Focused Head-to-Head Screen

## Decision Criteria

The comparison applied four criteria: distinctiveness in the stock-signals category, alignment with the desired British premium character, practical discoverability, and the absence of close finance, trading, fintech, market-data, or AI-technology uses.

## Focused Findings

| Candidate | Brand fit | Focused screen result | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thornwick** | Strong British performance-marque character; sharp and memorable. | Thornwick Capital Partners is an active private-equity firm; Thornwick AI is an active AI-systems company. | Do not adopt. The category proximity is too close. |
| **Greymark** | Modern, precise, and scalable; less heritage-led than Thornwick. | Greymark is an active provider of algorithmic trading systems, financial data pipelines, analytics platforms, and strategy automation. | Do not adopt. It is a direct same-category conflict. |

## Recommendation

Neither name should proceed to a rename or visual-identity stage. This focused screen shows why an initial broad scan is not sufficient: exact specialist searches revealed close active uses in the same market.

The brief remains sound—**classic British surname character, a strong visual silhouette, and modern analytical relevance**—but the next candidate set should use roots with deliberately lower public-web presence rather than familiar English components. Any future finalist should receive trademark, company-name, domain, and legal clearance before adoption.
