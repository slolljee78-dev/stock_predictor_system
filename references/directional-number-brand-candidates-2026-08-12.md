# Directional + Number Brand Candidates — Initial Screen

## Direction

This family preserves the concise, purposeful feel the user liked in **North Eight** and **Level Nine** while avoiding the exact terms **Level**, **Signal**, **Trade**, and **AI**. Each candidate combines a directional, spatial, or structural word with a restrained number. The intended product descriptor would provide clarity separately, for example: **“Evidence-led market intelligence”**.

## Screen limitation

These are creative candidates only. Initial public-web screening can identify obvious category conflicts, but it cannot provide trademark, company-name, domain, social-handle, or legal clearance.
