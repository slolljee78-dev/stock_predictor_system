## Understanding Trading Signals

### Signal Types

**🟢 Buy Signal**
- Indicates a potential buying opportunity
- Generated when technical indicators suggest upward momentum
- Confidence score shows the strength of the signal (50-100%)

**🔴 Sell Signal**
- Indicates a potential selling opportunity
- Generated when technical indicators suggest downward momentum
- Confidence score shows the strength of the signal (50-100%)

**⚪ Neutral**
- No clear buy or sell signal
- Market conditions are indecisive
- Hold current positions or wait for clearer signals

### Confidence Score

The confidence score (0-100%) indicates how strong the signal is:
- **50-60%**: Weak signal, use with caution
- **60-75%**: Moderate signal, consider alongside other analysis
- **75-90%**: Strong signal, good trading opportunity
- **90-100%**: Very strong signal, high conviction

## Technical Indicators

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- Measures momentum and overbought/oversold conditions
- Range: 0-100
- **Below 30**: Oversold (potential buy)
- **Above 70**: Overbought (potential sell)

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- Identifies trend changes and momentum
- Signals generated when MACD crosses the signal line
- Positive histogram: Bullish momentum
- Negative histogram: Bearish momentum

### Bollinger Bands
- Identifies volatility and support/resistance levels
- Three lines: Upper, Middle (SMA), Lower
- Price touching upper band: Potential resistance
- Price touching lower band: Potential support

### Moving Averages
- **SMA (Simple Moving Average)**: Average price over 20 periods
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)**: Weighted average giving more importance to recent prices
- Crossovers indicate potential trend changes

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Install
**Problem**: Install prompt doesn't appear
**Solution**: 
- Try using a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Ensure you're on a recent browser version
- Use iOS "Add to Home Screen" method as alternative

### Notifications Not Working
**Problem**: Not receiving alerts
**Solution**:
- Check notification permissions in app settings
- Ensure browser notifications are enabled
- Verify alert preferences are configured for your stocks
- Check notification center for missed alerts

### Signals Not Appearing
**Problem**: No trading signals generated
**Solution**:
- Ensure stock is added to watchlist
- Check alert preferences aren't set too high
- Wait for market hours (signals generated during trading)
- Verify stock has sufficient historical data

### Performance Issues
**Problem**: App is slow or unresponsive
**Solution**:
- Clear browser cache
- Close other browser tabs
- Reduce number of stocks in watchlist
- Try a different browser
- Check internet connection speed
