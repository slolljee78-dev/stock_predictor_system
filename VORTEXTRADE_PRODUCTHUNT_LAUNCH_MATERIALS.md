# Vortextrade ProductHunt Launch Materials

## ProductHunt Product Description

**Tagline:**
AI-powered stock signals with paper trading simulator. Beat the market with confidence.

**Full Description:**

Vortextrade is an AI-driven trading platform that generates real-time buy/sell signals for stocks, combined with a risk-free paper trading simulator to practice and validate strategies.

### Key Features:
- **AI Trading Signals**: Real-time buy/sell/hold signals powered by advanced technical analysis
- **Paper Trading Simulator**: Practice with $10,000 virtual capital, no real money at risk
- **15-Minute Intraday Data**: Real-time market data with technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands)
- **Backtesting Engine**: Test strategies on 5+ years of historical data with dividend/split handling
- **Community Challenges**: Monthly trading competitions with real prizes
- **Signal Accuracy Tracking**: See which signals perform best over time
- **Mobile-Optimized**: Trade on the go with responsive design
- **Free & Premium Tiers**: Start free, upgrade for advanced features

### Why Vortextrade?
- **No Real Money Risk**: Paper trading lets you learn before risking capital
- **AI-Powered Signals**: Machine learning analyzes 50+ technical indicators
- **Community-Driven**: Learn from other traders, compete in challenges
- **Completely Free**: Core features available at no cost
- **Educational**: 50+ blog articles on trading strategies and technical analysis

### Perfect For:
- Beginner traders learning technical analysis
- Experienced traders testing new strategies
- Anyone interested in stock market education
- Traders wanting to validate signals before trading real money

## FAQ for Launch

**Q: Is this a scam?**
A: No. We're completely free to start. No credit card required. All trading is simulated with virtual money.

**Q: Can I lose real money?**
A: No. The paper trading simulator uses virtual capital only. No real money is involved.

**Q: How accurate are the signals?**
A: Our top signals have 89% accuracy based on historical data. Results vary by stock and market conditions.

**Q: Is this for beginners or experienced traders?**
A: Both! Beginners can learn technical analysis. Experienced traders can test new strategies.

**Q: What's the catch?**
A: There is no catch. Core features are free forever. Premium tier ($9.99/month) adds advanced features.

**Q: How do I get started?**
A: Sign up, view signals, start paper trading. Takes 2 minutes.

**Q: Can I use this for real trading?**
A: Yes, but we recommend paper trading first to validate your strategy.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Not yet, but the website is fully mobile-optimized. Mobile apps coming soon.

**Q: How often are signals updated?**
A: Every 15 minutes during market hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM EST).

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. Premium members can export backtesting results and trading history.

**Q: Is there a community?**
A: Yes! Join monthly challenges, view leaderboards, and learn from other traders.
